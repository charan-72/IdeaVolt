IdeaVolt

Ideas hit at random — during a code review, on a walk, at 2 AM. Most disappear before they're written down.

This is a simple place to drop them. Open it, type it, save it. Come back later and they're still here.

No project boards, no workflows, no complexity. Just a place for ideas to land.
