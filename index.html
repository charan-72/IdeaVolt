How It Works

From what happened
to why it did.

No technical setup. No data team required. Splinth AI works with what you already have and gives you answers you can act on immediately.

1

Connect your tools

We plug into the systems you already use — your CRM, finance tools, operations platforms — and build a single, unified picture of how your business works. No migration. No disruption. No starting from scratch.

2

Ask a real question

Type what you want to know the same way you'd ask a colleague. "Why did churn spike in March?" or "What's slowing down our sales cycle?" No filters, no query language, no technical setup required.

3

Get a ranked explanation

Splinth AI doesn't just show you a chart. It tells you the top reasons behind the outcome, ordered by impact, with the evidence behind each one visible at a glance. No interpretation needed.

4

Act with confidence

Every answer comes with a clear trail back to the source. Share it with your team, take it into a meeting, or build on it, knowing exactly where it came from and why it's right.
